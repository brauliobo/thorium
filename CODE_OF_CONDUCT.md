# Alacrium Code of Conduct

The Alacrium community is committed to providing a respectful, welcoming, and
harassment-free environment for everyone participating in the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss code, behavior, and technical decisions rather than attacking people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect requests to stop unwelcome communication.
- Protect private or personally identifying information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or threats.
- Sexualized content or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Sustained disruption of project discussions.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Encouraging self-harm or violence.

Project maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently exclude participants whose behavior violates this policy.

Report conduct concerns privately to `brauliobo@gmail.com`. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.
